# Session reports and public case studies

## Before you start
Open the session in `apps/report/`. Check its Integrity section and the export `manifest.json`.

## Steps
1. Quote numbers from the report, with session id, plan id and adherence basis (live or review).
2. Separate: measured (from the export), operator report, interpretation.
3. Always state: one person, one session, ADC counts, prompted eye states, descriptive only.
4. Public by default: methods, code, aggregate numbers once approved. Private by default: raw recordings, identifiable media, health details.
5. Missing data are shown as missing (placeholders), never reconstructed.

## Stop if
A claim needs data that are not in the export, or publication has not been approved by the owner.

## Check
Every number on the page can be traced to a file and field.
